About

I like problems that sit between people and data — a shop owner who needs invoices that match the paper pad, an investor who wants to know why a stock moved, a student who learns better when a graph moves on screen.

My path has been hands-on. I learned HTML and CSS by building small pages, then JavaScript, then servers with Node and Express. Python pulled me toward APIs, Flask and FastAPI — and from there toward data: cleaning it, modelling it, and turning it into something a person can actually use.

Today I work across the stack, use AI as a serious engineering tool, and am building my foundation in data science. I care about clear interfaces, honest numbers and code that someone else can pick up.
